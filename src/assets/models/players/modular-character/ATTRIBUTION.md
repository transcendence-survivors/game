Animations: **Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard]** by Quaternius.

- `female-peasant.glb` combines the selected `Female_Peasant` mesh with the 43
  no-root-motion UAL2 clips plus `Walk_Loop` and `Walk_Formal_Loop` from UAL1.
  Its visible head is the `Superhero_Female_FullBody` base mesh, cropped to the
  head and combined with the source `Eyes`, `Eyebrows`, and
  `Hair_Long` meshes from Universal Base Characters. No head geometry
  is generated by the game.

The client uses `Idle_No_Loop` while stationary and the standard `Walk_Loop`
while moving. The no-root-motion library variants are intentional: the server
remains authoritative for the player's position.
