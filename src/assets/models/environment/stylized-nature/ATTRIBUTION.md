# Stylized Nature MegaKit

These models come from the [Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html) by Quaternius.

The pack is released under [Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The client uses a curated set of the pack's GLB exports:

- trees: common, pine and twisted tree variants;
- rocks: medium rocks and round pebbles;
- bushes: plain and flowering bushes;
- low vegetation: grass, fern, flowers, mushrooms and plants.

The source archive was converted from glTF to self-contained GLB files for the
Babylon.js client. The terrain decoration is placed deterministically from the
world seed and chunk coordinates.
